# Contributing to Daily AI Discovery

Thank you for contributing! We welcome community nominations for new free and freemium AI tools.

## Research Guidelines
Every nomination must satisfy our 9-Point Discovery Audit:
1. **Free or Freemium First**: Must offer a genuine free tier or free quota without requiring a credit card. No paid-only tools.
2. **No Duplicates**: Check `data/history.json` before submitting.
3. **Official Sources**: Quote official pricing, docs, or announcements.
4. **Transparent Limits**: State daily/monthly allowances and commercial usage limits clearly.
5. **Play Store Check**: Verify if an official Google Play app exists.
6. **Regional Accessibility**: Test or state availability for Pakistan and South Asian regions.
7. **No Benchmarks Inflation**: Report verified user adoption or official specs, never make up benchmark scores.
8. **Direct Links**: Provide official domain and sandbox try URLs.
9. **Permanent Records**: Submit a corresponding Markdown report in `/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Submission Process
1. Fork the repo and create your branch (`feature/nominate-tool-name`).
2. Test your tool submission via the built-in Research Engine (`[04] / Research Tool`).
3. Submit a Pull Request referencing the tool and date.
