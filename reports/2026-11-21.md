# Daily AI Discovery — Auphonic

**Featured discovery:** Auphonic — AI Audio Post-Production & Leveling

- **Date checked:** 2026-11-21 (Asia/Karachi)
- **Category:** Hidden gems from any AI category
- **Developer:** Auphonic GmbH
- **Verdict:** Auphonic is a browser-based AI audio post-production service for cleaning noisy recordings, balancing speech and music levels, removing filler pauses, and normalizing loudness. The free tier includes 2 hours of processing every month with the core audio algorithms and no card required, but free outputs contain an Auphonic jingle. It is best for speech-heavy audio and voiceovers, not a replacement for a DAW or creative music mastering.

## What it does

Auphonic applies machine-learning and audio-processing algorithms to uploaded audio or video. Its core tools include noise and reverb reduction, adaptive loudness leveling between speakers and music, filtering and voice enhancement, clipping/codec repair, filler-word and silence cutting, loudness normalization to standards such as EBU R128, and multitrack mixing. It is designed for podcasts, interviews, narration, education, radio and video dialogue; it can help prepare spoken intros, voice tags and voiceovers, but it is not a full music-production or sound-design workstation.

## Why it is worth trying

It can turn a rough speech recording into a more even, cleaner track without manually building a compressor, EQ and denoising chain. A practical test is to upload a short voiceover or spoken intro, compare the original and processed audio, and keep the final mix in your usual editor. The recurring 2-hour free allowance makes it useful for occasional work, provided the added jingle and privacy terms are acceptable.

## Free tier and exact limits

Auphonic Free provides 2 hours of processed audio per month, including its AI-based audio algorithms. The free allowance resets monthly and unused credits do not stack. Free users can use Intelligent Leveler, Noise & Reverb Reduction, Filtering & Enhancement, Loudness Specifications, and filler/silence cutting. The official homepage says free signup needs only an email address and no credit card. Free productions include an Auphonic jingle.

The free tier is genuinely usable for occasional post-production: it includes the core leveling, denoising, filtering and loudness algorithms, rather than being only a short trial. However, the two-hour allowance is measured by the duration of the input audio being processed, not by the time the service takes to run.

## Main limitations

Free multitrack productions are limited to under 20 minutes per production. Multilingual speech recognition, automatic show notes/chapters, watch folders and batch productions are not included in Free. The service bills by input-audio duration with a 3-minute minimum per production, so very short files can consume at least 3 minutes. Unused monthly free credits do not roll over. Free outputs include an Auphonic jingle. Auphonic is not a replacement for a DAW, detailed sound design or final creative music mastering. Privacy caveat: its Terms and Privacy Policy say uploaded media is stored for processing and may be retained in selected excerpts to improve algorithms; employees or contractors may listen to content for that purpose. Audio productions are normally deleted after 21 days and video after 7 days. Business customers can request a training opt-out; keep confidential client recordings out unless the data terms are acceptable.

Auphonic's public Terms of Service and registration page checked on 2026-11-21 did not identify Pakistan as a blocked country. ## Android status

An official Android companion app, Auphonic Edit, is listed on Google Play by Auphonic. It is primarily an offline recorder and non-destructive editor that can export recordings to the Auphonic web service; it is not the full AI post-production service in a native Android app. Google Play lists its last update as 2017-05-08. Pakistan-specific Play Store availability was not verified.

## Quality and adoption evidence

Vendor claim: Auphonic's homepage says it is used by 2M+ people and displays customer logos including BBC radio, Buzzsprout, iHeartRadio and MSNBC; this is company-reported adoption, not an independently audited user count. It also publishes testimonials from the Lex Fridman Podcast, Tom Scott, ORF and other production teams. Independent user-review signal: G2 lists 4.2/5 from 10 reviews, a small sample; reviewers praise ease of use and audio quality, while some mention pricing. Anecdotal community feedback: a May 2025 r/podcasting user said Auphonic performed best after trying five audio-cleanup tools; other Reddit users also report useful cleanup, but these are individual experiences. No standardized independent audio benchmark for Auphonic was located.
