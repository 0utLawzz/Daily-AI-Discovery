# Daily AI Discovery — 5 November 2026

## Suno — v6-mini (free-tier music generation)

**Developer:** Suno, Inc.  
**Category:** Voice, speech, music & audio AI  
**Date checked:** 5 November 2026  
**Verdict:** Strong for trying complete AI song ideas free. The daily generation allowance is useful, but new free accounts cannot rely on regular downloads and free songs are non-commercial.

### What it does
Suno turns a text prompt, mood, genre or lyrics into a complete song with vocals and instrumentation. The free tier uses **v6-mini**, a faster, lighter member of Suno's v6 model family.

### Why it is worth trying
- Create a vocal song or instrumental from a plain-language idea without music-production skills.
- Experiment with genre, mood, lyrics and song concepts quickly.
- Useful for sketches and inspiration; treat the output as a draft, not automatically as a release-ready master.

### Free tier and limits
- **50 credits refresh daily**; Suno says this is enough for up to **10 songs per day**.
- Free users get **v6-mini**. Suno says songs can be played and shared on its platform.
- **No commercial-use rights** for free-plan songs.
- **No monthly song downloads.** Users who joined before 3 September 2026 may have up to 7 lifetime trial downloads. Users joining on or after that date may only occasionally receive trial downloads, so a new account should not expect regular file exports.
- Free users share the creation queue and do not get stem separation.
- Optional paid upgrades exist for more downloads and commercial-use rights; they are not part of this recommendation.

### Signup and Pakistan availability
An account is required. Suno lists Google, Apple, Microsoft, Discord and phone-number sign-in. Suno says a free account can be started without a credit card. I found no official public country-availability list and did not perform a live Pakistan signup test, so **Pakistan-specific access is not confirmed**.

### Quality evidence
Suno's official v6 announcement describes v6-mini as the faster, more efficient free-access model; the v6 family supports full-song creation and generations up to 8 minutes. The official Google Play listing describes text-prompt, lyrics, humming and audio-input workflows. It showed a 4.8/5 rating from about 2.1 million reviews and 10M+ downloads when checked. That is an adoption signal, not a controlled model benchmark. No independent benchmark specific to v6-mini was found.
