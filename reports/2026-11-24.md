# Daily AI Discovery — 2026-11-24

## Vizard — AI Video Repurposing & Clip Maker

- **Category:** Video generation and editing AI
- **Developer:** Vizard, Corp.
- **Date checked:** 2026-11-24
- **Verdict:** Vizard repurposes long videos such as podcasts, interviews and webinars into short social clips by finding highlight moments, adding captions and reframing the footage. Its Free plan gives 60 credits each month (the help centre defines one credit as one minute processed), plus the editor and AI clipping. The trade-offs are a watermark, 720p exports, a 10-minute export cap and three-day project retention. It is useful for evaluating a repurposing workflow, but the free output is not clean, permanent client-ready production.

### What it does

Vizard is a browser-based AI video repurposing editor. Upload a video or paste a supported video link and it identifies candidate highlights, creates short clips, adds subtitles, reframes footage for vertical and other social formats, and lets you trim and polish the results. It is designed for existing long-form recordings—podcasts, interviews, webinars, tutorials and livestreams—not primarily for generating a new video from scratch.

### Why it is worth trying

It can reduce the manual work of scrubbing a long recording for shareable moments, then making separate crops and captions for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels or TikTok. The free allowance is enough to test the workflow on a few short source videos before deciding whether its clip selection and editing style suit your content.

### Free plan and exact limits

The official pricing page lists the Free plan at **$0 with 60 credits per month**. It includes AI clips, the full video editor, a private workspace, one connected social account and 720p exports.

Vizard's help centre defines **1 credit as 1 minute of video processed** for AI clipping or transcription. It says the same project is charged only once if both functions are used. On that documented baseline, 60 credits represent about 60 minutes of source footage per month.

**Practical note:** Export the clips you want to keep promptly. The free project expires after three days, and the exported video carries Vizard branding. These limits make the plan useful for evaluation, but less suitable for ongoing client delivery or clean branded publishing.

### Main limitations

- Free exports are watermarked and limited to 720p.
- Individual exports are capped at 10 minutes.
- Free projects expire after three days.
- The free upload allowance is only 60 credits per month; long recordings use it quickly.
- AI-selected moments, captions and reframing need a human review. It is a first-pass repurposing tool, not a full multi-track professional editor.
- Vizard's Terms of Service page shows an update date of 31 October 2022 and describes machine-learning use of usage data and user-provided content for testing, tuning and improving its service. Review the current terms and privacy policy before uploading confidential, client-owned or sensitive footage.

### Quality evidence

- **Vendor information:** Vizard's official product page documents highlight extraction, automatic captions, reframing and social-format editing. Its pricing and help pages provide the free-plan and credit limits. These are vendor descriptions, not independent performance proof.
- **Independent hands-on review:** CreatorIntelHQ describes testing signup, upload, clipping, captions, reframing, editor access, downloads and watermark behaviour on short and 10-minute 1080p footage in August 2026. The reviewer found captions mostly correct but with some word errors, and said 9:16 reframing did not always keep the speaker centred. The review discloses affiliate links.
- **Anecdotal community feedback:** The Google Play listing showed mixed individual feedback: some users appreciated the free workflow, while one reviewer complained about slow processing. These comments are anecdotal and not representative testing.
- **Testing disclosure:** This report did not perform a hands-on generation test.
