# Daily AI Discovery — Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)

- **Date checked:** 2026-11-08
- **Category:** General-purpose, research, reasoning and multimodal AI
- **Developer:** Google LLC
- **Verdict:** A strong free, source-grounded research workspace: ask questions across PDFs, web pages, YouTube and other material with inline citations, then turn sources into briefs, study aids and audio. Standard access includes 100 notebooks and 50 sources per notebook; AI usage is compute-based and its exact prompt quota is unpublished.

## What it does

Gemini Notebook is Google's source-grounded research assistant, renamed from NotebookLM on 16 July 2026. Add PDFs, websites, YouTube videos, audio files, Google Docs or Google Slides to a notebook. Ask questions about selected sources and receive answers with inline citations that link back to the material. You can also discover and import web sources, then turn your collection into briefings, study guides, Audio Overviews, Video Overviews, mind maps, quizzes, flashcards, infographics and slide decks.

Google introduced Deep Research and additional source types in its November 2025 update. The current product is a research workspace, not simply another open-ended chatbot.

## Why it is worth trying

It is most useful when an answer must be grounded in a known set of documents: compare reports, study a collection of papers, extract facts from product documentation or prepare a briefing from source material. Instead of trusting a free-form answer, open the inline citation and inspect the passage yourself. The same notebook can then produce study and presentation formats from the source collection.

This is a different use case from the previously featured Duck.ai: Gemini Notebook is centered on a user's own research corpus and citations, not switching among general chat models.

## Free tier and published limits

Google's plan comparison identifies a free **Standard** tier available with a personal Google account. The published Standard limits are:

- **100 notebooks per user**
- **50 sources per notebook**

The important caveat is that Google now describes AI usage limits as **compute-based**. The amount available depends on prompt complexity, selected models and features, chat length and other factors. The current help page does **not** publish a fixed number of free prompts or generations per day. It says usage limits refresh every five hours until the weekly limit is reached, and users can see remaining usage in Settings → Usage or in the chat/Studio interface. Limits may change.

Paid Google AI plans offer higher limits and additional capabilities, but the free Standard tier is usable without buying an upgrade. No paid-plan price or Pakistan-specific payment acceptance is asserted here.

## Main limitations and cautions

- The exact Standard prompt and generation quota is not public, so heavy users may hit a limit earlier than expected.
- The 50-source cap applies to each free notebook; separate notebooks do not automatically share source context.
- The Android app is an early version. It supports PDF, website, YouTube, audio and copied-text sources, but Google's help page says notes, mind maps, reports, data tables and chat configuration/analytics are not yet supported in the app.
- Google warns that AI-generated chat responses and artifacts may contain inaccuracies. Citations make checking easier but do not prove that an answer correctly interprets its source.
- For personal Google accounts, Google says uploaded data is not used to train Gemini Notebook unless the user submits feedback; when feedback is submitted, the full interaction context may be reviewed. Avoid uploading sensitive material unless the applicable terms and handling are acceptable.

## Pakistan availability

Google's official supported-country list for browser access includes **Pakistan**. The official Android rollout list also includes Pakistan. A personal Google Account and sign-in are required, and age-of-consent/age-verification requirements apply. Work or school accounts may require an administrator to enable access.

This verifies listed regional availability, not a live account-level sign-in test. The free Standard tier is available without a paid AI plan. Paid-plan pricing and payment acceptance for a Pakistan billing profile were not verified.

## Android app

**Verified:** Official Gemini Notebook app by Google LLC on Google Play.

- Android 10 or later is required.
- Pakistan is listed in Google's Android rollout countries.
- The app supports asking questions about sources, Audio Overviews, flashcards/quizzes, infographics and slide decks, but its source types and features remain narrower than the web version.

## Quality and adoption evidence

**Vendor-reported:** Google's 16 July 2026 announcement renaming NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook said the product had more than **30 million users** and was used by over **600,000 organizations**. These are Google's own figures, not independently audited adoption statistics.

**Platform evidence:** The official Google Play listing showed **50M+ downloads** and a **4.7-star** rating from approximately **354K reviews** when checked on 2026-11-08. Store ratings and download bands are useful adoption signals, not controlled quality benchmarks.

**Independent evaluation:** Benchmarks.law's early-2025 human-judged comparison tested six assistants on 18 real in-house legal extraction tasks. The then-free NotebookLM passed 14 of 18 tasks (77.8%). This is a narrow, small-sample legal-document test of the earlier product, not a broad benchmark of the current Gemini Notebook release. The same study documented failure cases, so it should not be read as proof of legal reliability.

**Anecdotal community feedback:** A June 2026 Reddit discussion includes a user describing cross-referencing many documents as a useful part of their workflow. This is one user's report and is not independently verified.

No independent broad benchmark of the current 2026 Gemini Notebook release was located during this check.
