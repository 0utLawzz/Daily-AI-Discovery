# Daily AI Discovery — 2026-11-23

**Category:** Image generation and editing AI  
**Product:** Ideogram — AI Image Generator & Editor (4.6)  
**Developer:** Ideogram, Inc.  
**Date checked:** 2026-11-23

## Verdict

Ideogram is a strong candidate when the image needs its own headline, label or typography—not just a background picture. Its Free plan is genuinely usable for testing, but the weekly allowance is small, free generations are public, and higher-quality export options are restricted. The newly released 4.6 model is especially interesting for precise iterative editing, although its exact app-credit cost is quoted per run.

## What it does

Ideogram creates images from text prompts and reference media, with a particular focus on readable lettering for posters, logos, packaging, signage and social graphics. It also offers image-editing apps for precise changes, background/object removal, upscaling, ad resizing and vector artwork.

Ideogram 4.6 was released on November 19, 2026. The vendor describes it as a precise multi-step editing model with output up to 4K, up to five reference images in Generate, and optional masks for focused Precise Edit workflows. You can make a change, inspect the result, and continue editing the same image rather than restarting.  
Official sources: https://ideogram.ai/ and https://ideogram.ai/models/4.6/

## Why it is worth trying

- Useful for posters, book covers, product labels, packaging and social posts where wording is part of the visual.
- Supports focused edits to existing images, including product-color and detail changes.
- Has a real $0 plan, so a small test does not require buying a subscription.

It is a specialist design helper, not a promise that every word or detail will be perfect. Proofread generated text before publishing.

## Free tier and limits

**Free plan: $0 — 30 credits per week**, according to the official Plans and Credits guide: https://docs.ideogram.ai/plans-and-billing/available-plans

Sign-in eligibility needs care. The official documentation is inconsistent about Microsoft sign-in: the FAQ says Google, Apple or Microsoft accounts receive weekly credits, while the Quick Start page's note says Google and Apple receive 30 credits and Microsoft receives none. The same Quick Start page says email/password accounts receive no free credits. For the clearest documented route, use Google or Apple sign-in.

For context, the published credit table for Ideogram 4.5 and 4.0 lists 3, 6 or 10 credits per image depending on quality. Thirty credits would therefore cover approximately 10 low-cost, 5 medium or 3 high-quality images at those older-model rates. **Do not assume those rates apply to 4.6:** Ideogram says the app's Generate button displays the exact credit quote for the selected model and settings.

Important free-plan restrictions:

- Everything generated on the Free plan is public.
- Free downloads are original-size JPEG only.
- PNG, WebP and upscaled downloads require a paid plan or Pay as you go credits.
- Video and some premium apps require a paid plan or purchased credits.
- The 4.6 app-credit amount is not one fixed published number; check the quote before running it.
- Ideogram's FAQ says text rendering is strongest in English and other Latin-script languages. Non-Latin scripts may come out incorrectly or not at all, so Urdu/Arabic lettering should be added or checked separately.

Official sources: https://docs.ideogram.ai/quick-start-guide and https://ideogram.ai/features/faq/

## Pakistan availability

No Pakistan-specific signup prohibition was found in the official public pages reviewed. However, Pakistan-based registration and image generation were not hands-on tested, so this is not a guarantee of local access.

The free plan does not require a paid checkout. Paid billing is listed in USD and processed by card through Stripe; acceptance of Pakistani cards and any bank conversion fees were not verified. Use Google or Apple sign-in for the clearest documented free-plan eligibility.

## Quality evidence

### Vendor claims

Ideogram markets its platform around prompt fidelity, readable text and image editing. Its 4.6 page reports a first-place score of 1,892 on the Rapidata Multi-step Image Edit benchmark, using a November 17, 2026 leaderboard snapshot and a specified high-quality Precise Edit configuration. This is a vendor-published benchmark result and should not be treated as a universal guarantee.  
Source: https://ideogram.ai/models/4.6/

### Independent evidence

Contra Labs ran a blind evaluation with 10 working designers, four image models and 240 images across 60 prompts. Ideogram 4.0 won 47.9% of typography matchups and received 3.92/5 for typographic craft. This is useful independent evidence for the product family, but it tested Ideogram 4.0—not the newly released 4.6—and the sample is relatively small.  
Source: https://contralabs.com/research/ideogram-v4-typography-leader

### Anecdotal community feedback

A June 2026 Reddit user shared poster recreations made with Ideogram 4 and praised its control over layout, text styles and object placement, while noting that the results required detailed bounding boxes and extensive prompting. This is an individual experience, not a controlled test.  
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1u6sld9/nothing_but_prompts_ideogram_4_has_scary_control/
