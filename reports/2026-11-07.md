# Daily AI Discovery — 7 November 2026

## Duck.ai — Private multi-model AI chat

**Developer:** DuckDuckGo  
**Category:** Hidden gems across any AI category  
**Date checked:** 7 November 2026  
**Verdict:** A useful, often-overlooked no-signup way to compare several major AI models in one privacy-focused chat. The free tier is genuinely usable for light everyday work, but its daily cap is not published as a number.

### What it does
Duck.ai is a browser-based chat interface for third-party AI models. Its current free model list includes **Claude Haiku 5.5, Mistral Small 4, GPT-6 Luna, GPT-5.4 mini, gpt-oss-120b and Gemma 4 31B**. You can ask questions, draft or rewrite text, summarize, and switch models within the same service.

### Why it is worth trying
- Start chatting without creating an account.
- Choose from multiple model families rather than being locked to one assistant.
- Use **Second Opinion** to send the same prompt to another model and compare answers.
- The official Q3 2026 update also documents model selection, reasoning controls, web search and image creation in the chat flow.

These are practical product-quality indicators, not benchmark claims. No independent benchmark of Duck.ai as a wrapper was found; answer quality depends on the underlying model selected.

### Free tier and limits
- **Price:** Free; no account required.
- **Usage:** A daily limit applies, but DuckDuckGo does **not** publish a fixed number of messages or tokens.
- **Attachments:** Image and PDF uploads work with supported models. Free PDF uploads are limited to **15 pages**.
- **Not supported:** Spreadsheet uploads.
- **Optional upgrade:** DuckDuckGo subscription access raises usage limits and unlocks additional models; it is not required for the free tier.

### Privacy and signup
DuckDuckGo says it removes identifying metadata such as the user's IP address before forwarding prompts, and that providers agree not to train on Duck.ai chats. Provider-specific short-term caching and limited legal/safety exceptions are documented, so do not treat this as a place for confidential client or business data.

### Pakistan availability
No account is required for the basic free service, and Duck.ai works in a web browser. DuckDuckGo does not publish a country-by-country availability list for free access; a live Pakistan test was not performed. Pakistan is not included in the current official list of regions where the **paid subscription** is offered, but that does not prove free access is blocked.

### Android availability
**Official Android app verified:** DuckDuckGo Private Browser. Duck.ai can be accessed through the browser; this is **not** a separate standalone Duck.ai app.
