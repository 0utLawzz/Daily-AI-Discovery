## What it does

PixVerse is an AI video-creation workspace available on the web and Android. Its core workflows include:

- **Text-to-video:** turn a written prompt into a short generated scene.
- **Image-to-video:** animate a still image into a moving clip.
- **Video editing and extension:** refine selected video sections, extend a scene, and create transitions between frames.
- **Motion and reference controls:** guide character movement with a reference motion clip and use reference images to guide characters, objects or style.
- **Creator tools:** templates/effects, talking avatars and lip-sync, music-video creation, and sound effects.

The feature list is vendor-described. Model and feature access can vary by plan, app version and region. This research did not run a hands-on generation, so output quality and render speed were not personally tested.

## Why it is worth trying

PixVerse combines generation and practical short-video workflows in one place. A useful first experiment is to upload a non-sensitive photo, describe a simple camera movement, and compare the result with a text-only prompt. That lets a creator test image animation, templates and editing before paying for a plan.

The strongest case is rapid prototyping for social clips, storyboards, music-video concepts and visual experiments. It should not be treated as a free commercial deliverable tool because the free-use terms restrict outputs to non-commercial purposes.

## Free tier and exact limits

The official subscription page lists the **Basic** plan at **US$0** with:

| Item | Basic free plan |
|---|---:|
| Initial credits | 60 |
| Daily credits | 30 |
| Daily reset | 00:00 UTC |
| Unused daily credits | Expire at the end of the UTC day |
| Maximum video resolution | Up to 540p |
| Concurrent generations | 2 |
| Watermark-free export | Not listed as included; watermark-free is a paid-plan benefit |
| Commercial use | Not allowed for free-service outputs under the Terms |

Credits are not a fixed number of videos. Consumption changes with the selected model, resolution, duration, audio and other generation settings. The FAQ says the current cost is shown in the creation interface; regenerating charges credits again, while failed generations are automatically refunded. Therefore, do not interpret 30 daily credits as 30 daily videos.

For context, the official pricing page showed the Standard plan at US$10 month-to-month or US$96 billed annually (displayed as US$8/month when billed yearly). This is optional paid pricing, not part of the free tier.

## Main limits and cautions

1. **Low free resolution:** Basic is capped at 540p; higher output tiers are paid.
2. **Limited credits:** 60 initial plus 30 daily credits, with daily credits expiring at 00:00 UTC.
3. **No watermark-free benefit on Basic:** paid plans explicitly advertise watermark-free exports.
4. **Non-commercial free use:** the Terms limit free-service outputs to non-commercial use. Paid commercial rights apply only under the stated conditions and to PixVerse-developed models; third-party model outputs follow the relevant third-party rules.
5. **Privacy:** the Terms describe use of information content in aggregated and anonymized form to improve and provide the service, including model training/optimization, unless an opt-out is made available. Avoid uploading confidential client or personal material unless the applicable settings and terms have been reviewed.
6. **Variable regional features:** model and feature availability can vary by country or region.

In a 2026-03-12 company announcement, PixVerse reported more than **100 million users across 175 countries**, **2.1 billion videos generated**, and **16 million monthly active users**. These figures are vendor-reported and were not independently audited in this research.

### Anecdotal community feedback
