# Daily AI Discovery — 26 November 2026

## Moises — AI Stem Separation & Music Practice App

- **Category:** Voice, speech, music and audio AI
- **Developer:** Moises Systems, Inc.
- **Date checked:** 2026-11-26
- **Verdict:** A useful free starter for musicians who want to isolate vocals/instruments and practise with songs they own. Its strongest advantage is the combination of stem separation, chord/key/BPM detection and tempo/pitch controls. The free quota is small, and the separation is not artefact-free.

## What it does

Moises is an AI-powered music practice and production workspace for web, Android, iOS and desktop. It can:

- Separate an existing recording into **2 stems** (vocals and instrumental) or **4 stems** (vocals, drums, bass and other).
- Detect the song's key and BPM.
- Display detected chords in sync with playback.
- Generate a synchronized metronome/click track.
- Change pitch and tempo so a musician can practise a difficult part or sing in a more suitable key.
- Mute or solo separated parts to create backing tracks, karaoke versions or practice mixes.

The newer browser-based Moises Studio adds multitrack recording, arrangement, collaboration and AI-generated supporting stems. Moises is primarily a tool for working with recordings already in hand; it is not simply a prompt-to-complete-song generator.

## Why it is worth trying

It brings several useful musician workflows into one place. A guitarist can lower the guitar or isolate the rhythm section, a drummer can practise against a drumless backing track, and a singer can lower vocals or adjust the key. The free allowance is enough to test a few short tracks before deciding whether the practice workflow is useful.

## Free tier and exact limits

The current official Free offer lists:

- **5 AI audio separations per month.**
- **Up to 5 minutes per uploaded file** for Free stem separation.
- Two preset separation options:
  - 2 stems: vocals and instrumental.
  - 4 stems: vocals, drums, bass and other.
- Free pitch/tempo controls and key/BPM detection.
- Chord detection, Smart Metronome and lyrics transcription are limited to the **first minute** of a song on Free.

The Help Center confirms the five-file monthly upload cap. Deleting an uploaded file does not restore the slot; the counter resets at the start of the next calendar month.

**AI Studio credit discrepancy:** Moises' current promotional page says Free includes 60 monthly Gen-AI credits, while its Help Center credit explainer says Free users receive 10 AI Studio credits per month. These pages conflict, so this report does not treat that separate generation-credit amount as a dependable quota. Check the account's in-app credit balance before relying on it.

## Main limitations and practical cautions

1. Five monthly upload slots are restrictive for regular use, and deleted files still count.
2. Free files are limited to five minutes each.
3. Free separation is limited to 2- or 4-stem presets. Individual instrument choices such as guitar, piano and strings require Premium or Pro.
4. Chords, Smart Metronome and lyrics transcription are available only for the first minute on Free.
5. Free files are retained for two months from the last actual login and may be deleted after that period.
6. WAV export through the web/desktop app is Premium-only; mobile export options include MP3 and M4A.
7. Spotify, Apple Music and other streaming-service URLs are not accepted for stem separation. Use an audio file you own or have permission to modify. A streaming subscription alone does not grant that right.

## Quality and adoption evidence

### Vendor claims

Moises says more than 80 million musicians use its tools worldwide. Its official stem-separation page describes 27 stem targets at the highest subscription tier and says user uploads are not used to train its models. These are company claims.

### Independent evidence

MusicRadar's 7 January 2026 comparison tested 11 stem-separation tools using the same three mixed songs at the highest available settings. It used listening tests and phase-cancellation checks. Moises received an honourable mention rather than a top-five score: reviewers found drum and bass extraction good, but vocal extraction showed warbling artefacts, clicks and spill from other stems. This is a useful warning that separated stems are not always clean enough for professional release without checking and editing.

### Anecdotal community feedback

Recent Google Play reviews include one user praising vocal isolation, while other users complained about paid feature gates, importing personal music files and subscription-state prompts. These are individual reports and should not be treated as controlled quality tests.

**Testing disclosure:** No account was created and no audio was processed for this report. The assessment is based on public official documentation, the Google Play listing and published third-party testing.
