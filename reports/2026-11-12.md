# Daily AI Discovery — 2026-11-12

## Discovery
- **Name:** ElevenLabs — AI Voice Generator (Eleven v4)
- **Category:** Voice, speech, music and audio AI
- **Developer:** ElevenLabs (Google Play publisher: Eleven Labs Inc.)
- **Date checked:** 2026-11-12 (Asia/Karachi)
- **Verdict:** A strong voiceover and text-to-speech workspace with a usable free tier: 10,000 shared credits per month (about 10 minutes of standard TTS). Free output is non-commercial and requires attribution; voice cloning and commercial rights require a paid plan.

## What it does
ElevenLabs turns scripts into expressive, downloadable speech for narration, social videos, explainers and podcasts. The wider platform also includes speech-to-text, voice design, sound effects, music and dubbing tools. This discovery focuses on voice generation and voiceover, which is distinct from Suno's AI song-generation use case already present in the history ledger.

The workflow is straightforward: enter or paste text, choose a voice and model, adjust delivery settings, generate, then download the audio. ElevenLabs says its latest v4 family supports expressive delivery controls and multilingual generation. The company documentation cautions that the selected voice has a major effect on accent and pronunciation; use a voice trained for the target language where possible.

## Why it is worth trying
- Create narration without recording a microphone take.
- Draft voiceovers for short videos, explainers, demos and podcasts.
- Compare voices, pacing and emotional delivery before settling on a style.
- Use the same account across the web product and official Android app.

It is worth testing with a short sample in the intended language first. This report did not create or listen to a generated sample, so no hands-on output claim is made.

## Free tier and exact limits
The official pricing page currently lists:
- **Price:** Free, $0/month.
- **Monthly allowance:** **10,000 credits**.
- **Studio:** 3 projects.
- **Voice Design:** 3 custom voice slots; these are for designed voices, not voice cloning.
- **Approximate TTS volume:** the pricing comparison estimates about **10 minutes**. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed runtime.
- **Credit calculation:** for V2 Multilingual TTS, 1 text character = 1 credit. Costs vary by model.
- **Shared pool:** credits are shared across the platform. The pricing FAQ lists approximately 330 credits/minute for speech-to-text, 900 credits/minute for Eleven Music, 200 credits per sound-effect generation, 1,000 credits/minute for voice changer/isolator, and 2,000 credits/minute for automatic dubbing with watermark (higher for other dubbing options).
- **Reset/rollover:** free credits reset at the start of the billing cycle and do not roll over. Paid-plan rollover rules do not apply to Free.

The free plan includes text-to-speech, speech-to-text, sound effects, voice design, music and other creative features, but all consume the same monthly credit balance.

## Main limits and restrictions
- Free-plan content is for **non-commercial use only** and requires attribution to ElevenLabs when shared.
- The Free plan does **not** include a commercial license.
- Instant Voice Cloning starts on Starter; Professional Voice Cloning starts on Creator.
- The 10,000 credits are shared across products, so trying Music, dubbing, transcription or effects reduces the amount left for TTS.
- Credit cost varies by model and task; each generation consumes credits.
- Output pronunciation and accent depend substantially on the selected voice and its training language.

The pricing page showed Starter at a temporary first-month promotion followed by $6/month at the time checked. Promotions can change; confirm the current checkout price. A paid upgrade is not required to try the free tier.

## Pakistan signup and availability
ElevenLabs' published blocked-country list names Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Crimea, and the Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts of Ukraine; Pakistan is not listed. The billing documentation says new signups are automatically assigned to the Free tier, so a payment method is not required to try it.

The company says its official mobile app is available worldwide. However, Pakistan-specific account signup and the Play Store listing's availability in the Pakistan storefront were not independently tested. Paid checkout lists credit card, Apple Pay and Google Pay; acceptance of Pakistan-issued cards and local wallet availability were not verified.

## Quality and adoption evidence
**Vendor claim:** ElevenLabs describes Eleven v4 as its most expressive speech model. This is the vendor's own product positioning, not independent proof.

**Independent evidence:** The [Artificial Analysis Provider Voice Arena](https://artificialanalysis.ai/embed/text-to-speech-leaderboard/leaderboard/provider-voice) snapshot checked on 2026-11-12 ranked:
- Eleven v4: **#1**, Elo **1338 ± 15**, **2,722** samples.
- Eleven v4 Turbo: **#2**, Elo **1337 ± 16**, **2,426** samples.

This arena is based on crowdsourced preference comparisons. The confidence intervals overlap substantially, so the one-place difference should not be interpreted as a meaningful gap. Results are a leaderboard snapshot and can change.

**Anecdotal feedback/adoption:** Google Play showed 10M+ downloads. Recent individual reviews include positive comments about natural voice quality and at least one report of generation being blocked by abuse detection despite unused credits; the developer replied that it would investigate. These are individual user reports, not representative research. The Play Store's rating figures were internally inconsistent at the time checked, as noted above.

A separate [Model Report Card analysis](https://modelreportcard.com/elevenlabs-v4-75-percent-preference-test) notes that ElevenLabs' own “about 75% listener preference” claim does not publish the sample size, grader pool or confidence intervals. That figure should therefore be treated as a vendor-reported result, not an independent benchmark.
