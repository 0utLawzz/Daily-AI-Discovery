# Daily AI Discovery — OpenRouter

**Featured discovery:** OpenRouter — Multi-model AI API Gateway

- **Date checked:** 2026-11-20 (Asia/Karachi)
- **Category:** Coding and developer AI
- **Developer:** OpenRouter, Inc.
- **Verdict:** A developer-focused gateway that exposes hundreds of third-party AI models through one OpenAI-compatible API and a browser chat. Its free tier lists 25+ free models and 4 providers, with free-model requests capped at 20 per minute and 50 per day. The main trade-off is that free-model availability and quality vary by upstream provider; it is best for prototyping and model evaluation, not production reliability.

## What it does

OpenRouter provides one account, model catalogue and API endpoint for calling models from many providers. Developers can compare and switch models without rewriting their integration, use an OpenAI-compatible chat-completions API, browse model pricing and provider availability, and use the browser chat for manual experiments. It is an AI infrastructure/API gateway, not an IDE code-completion assistant.

## Why it is worth trying

Useful for developers prototyping an AI feature, comparing free models, or adding model choice to an app without integrating each provider separately. The browser chat offers a no-code way to try available free models; API use requires creating an account and API key. Its use case is distinct from code completion (GitHub Copilot) and pull-request review (CodeRabbit) already in the history.

## Free tier and exact limits

Official pricing page lists 25+ free models, 4 free providers, browser chat and API access, activity logs, up to 5 workspaces, and no platform fee. Free model variants (model IDs ending in :free) have a $0 model price and are capped at 20 requests/minute and 50 requests/day for accounts that have purchased less than $10 in credits. Free model availability changes over time. API requests require an account/API key; browser chat is available through the website.

The current free-model cap is **20 requests per minute and 50 requests per day** for accounts with less than $10 in lifetime purchased credits. The higher 1,000/day cap requires a paid credit purchase and is not part of the free tier. The free model catalogue is dynamic; check the live list before building against a particular model.

## Main limitations

Free plan excludes paid models and advanced controls such as budgets/spend controls, preferred-provider routing, prompt caching, management API keys, data-policy routing, SSO and private endpoints. Free model endpoints can be rate-limited or unavailable by upstream providers; failed requests may count toward the daily cap. Buying at least $10 in credits raises the free-model daily cap to 1,000 requests but is a paid top-up and is not required for the basic free tier. Provider/model terms, context limits, latency and data handling differ; review each model's terms before use. Do not send confidential client or personal data unless the selected model/provider data terms are acceptable.

## Pakistan signup and availability

OpenRouter's Terms of Service checked on 2026-11-20 require users to be at least 18 and do not explicitly name Pakistan as excluded. They warn that individual model providers may impose country/region restrictions, so availability can vary by model. Pakistan signup was not independently tested. The free tier lists no payment method requirement; paid credit checkout acceptance for Pakistani cards or local payment methods was not verified. Free-model availability in the browser/API should therefore be treated as subject to provider restrictions.

## Android status

No official first-party OpenRouter Android app was verified in Google Play research. Third-party Android clients that connect to OpenRouter exist, but they are independent apps and are not endorsed or published by OpenRouter. Use the browser chat or API instead.

No official Android Play Store URL is supplied because no first-party app was verified. Third-party clients are not official OpenRouter apps.

## Quality and adoption evidence

Vendor-reported adoption: OpenRouter's homepage displayed 800T+ monthly tokens, 10M+ users, 600+ models and 80+ providers on 2026-11-20; these are company claims, not independently audited. Vendor usage signal: its free-model rankings are based on token volume processed through OpenRouter over the trailing seven days, which measures platform usage rather than model quality. Independent research: a 2026 NYU Stern thesis analyses weekly OpenRouter model-market data from 2025-08-24 to 2026-02-22, evidence that the marketplace is used as a research dataset, not a product quality benchmark. Anecdotal community feedback is mixed: Reddit users report useful experimentation but also intermittent provider rate limits, latency and inconsistent free endpoints. No standardized independent benchmark of OpenRouter itself was verified.
