- **Category:** Hidden gems from any AI category

- **Verdict:** A useful browser-based visual communication tool that turns selected text into editable diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, data charts and infographics. Its free plan includes 500 AI credits per week plus unlimited manual editing and PNG/PDF exports; free visuals carry Napkin branding and PPT/SVG exports are limited.

## What it does
Napkin AI converts text or imported material into editable visual explanations such as flowcharts, mind maps, process diagrams, data charts and infographics. Users paste or import content, select text, generate visual alternatives, then adjust wording, colors, fonts and elements. It supports PPT, DOC, PDF, HTML and Markdown imports; PNG and PDF exports are unlimited on Free. Napkin Slides is also included across plans, with the first presentation of up to 20 slides free of AI-credit charges while it is the user's only presentation.

## Why it is worth trying
It is a low-friction way to turn existing process notes, SOPs, workflow descriptions, reports and presentation text into visual explanations without starting from a blank design canvas or learning a diagramming package. Unlike general image generators, the output is intended to remain editable as a diagram rather than a flattened decorative image.

## Free tier and exact limits
Free forever: 500 AI credits per week. Napkin says visual generation costs approximately 1 credit per word selected; complex outputs or advanced actions may cost differently. Free includes unlimited manual visual editing, unlimited supported file imports subject to size/processing limits, unlimited PNG and PDF exports, built-in styles/fonts/icons, and Napkin branding on visuals. PPT and SVG exports are available on Free but capped; the exact free export count is not published. The first presentation of up to 20 slides is credit-free while it is the user's only presentation.

Additional usage details from Napkin's Help Center:
- Free credits refresh weekly and do not roll over.
- A visual normally costs about 1 credit per selected word.
- Slide generation normally costs 100 credits per slide; the first presentation of up to 20 slides is free while it is the account's only presentation.
- Applying a Visual Effect can use up to 100 credits per visual or slide.
- Direct editing of text, colors, fonts and elements does not use credits.
- Free users can export PNG and PDF without a stated cap. PPT and SVG exports are available on Free but limited; Napkin does not publish the exact free export count.

## Main limitations and privacy
Free credits refresh weekly and do not roll over. Generating a visual costs about 1 credit per selected word; slide generation normally costs 100 credits per slide, agent requests vary, and Visual Effects can use up to 100 credits per visual/slide. Direct edits to text, colors, fonts and elements do not use credits. Free outputs carry Napkin branding; PPT/SVG export is capped with no exact cap published. Individual visual generation requires a computer: on mobile, users can view work and create/edit presentations but cannot generate individual visuals. Self-serve accounts may contribute anonymized interaction data (such as selected text and visual choices) to product/model improvement unless the user opts out in settings. Napkin's terms say visual content itself is not used for AI model training; outputs can still be inaccurate or unsuitable and should be reviewed.
