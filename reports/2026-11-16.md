# Daily AI Discovery — Photoroom

- **Date checked:** 2026-11-16 (Asia/Karachi)
- **Category:** Image generation and editing AI
- **Developer:** Photoroom SAS (France)
- **Verdict:** Worth trying for personal, non-commercial product-photo cleanup and listing-image preparation. It is not a general-purpose text-to-image replacement: its differentiator is product photography, background removal/replacement, retouching and commerce-ready output. The free tier has a real export allowance, but its non-commercial restriction is decisive for business use.

## What it does

Photoroom edits existing photos. Its tools include automatic background removal and replacement, retouch/erase, AI expand, image enhancement, resizing, preset shadows, AI-generated product scenes, product staging, fashion models and batch workflows. The strongest use case is turning a simple product photo into a cleaner listing visual for marketplaces or social catalogues.

The vendor's product pages and Play listing describe background removal, replacement, batch editing, product scenes and listing-ready exports. Feature access depends on the Space plan; not every advanced tool is available on Free.

## Why it is worth trying

For a single item photo, a user can remove the original background, place the product on a cleaner scene, retouch distractions and resize the result for a listing. This is a narrower product-photography workflow than the general image-generation tools already present in the discovery history.

The free plan is suitable for testing with personal or non-commercial images. **Do not use the Free plan for commercial BrandEx/client work:** Photoroom's terms explicitly limit Free Accounts to personal, non-commercial purposes.

## Free tier and exact limits

Photoroom's official Help Center says a Free Space is free but has limited features, lower AI-credit and export limits.

- The official export table lists **25 exports in its weekly column and 100 exports in its monthly column** for Free.
- The same article says Free-account renewal is monthly, based on the date and time the account was created. Because the table has both weekly and monthly columns, check the in-app usage meter rather than assuming a weekly reset for every Free account.
- Free AI generation is described as a low allowance, but the exact AI-credit number is not published in the pages checked.
- Free Utilities (Background Remover, AI Expand, Image Enhancer, preset AI Shadows, Resize and Retouch) have a daily limit, but the exact numeric threshold is not published.
- Free access is personal and non-commercial only.
- Some features are unavailable on Free. Advanced AI features, higher generation limits, Batch Mode and API access require paid access.

**Paid-plan reference:** the official pricing page displayed Pro at US$7.50/month billed yearly, Max at US$20.99/month billed yearly and Ultra at US$82.50/month billed yearly when checked. Prices, billing periods and local taxes may vary by region; these are not required to use the Free Space.

## Main limitations and privacy caveat

1. The free account is **not licensed for commercial use**.
2. Free export allowance is capped; downloads, saves, shares and other external image outputs count against the Space's export limit.
3. Free AI credits and daily utility limits are not published as exact numeric quotas.
4. Some advanced AI tools and batch workflows are paid-only.
5. Photoroom's Terms and Privacy Policy say uploaded images may be used to improve/train its products and models. An opt-out is available in account Data Control settings, but the Privacy Policy says opting out does not undo processing already carried out.

Avoid uploading confidential client images, private documents or any image for which the account holder lacks the necessary rights/consents.

## Pakistan signup, access and payment

Photoroom documents sign-in by email or Google, Apple and Facebook. The public terms checked on 2026-11-16 did not name Pakistan as an excluded country, but Pakistan-based signup and regional feature access were not independently tested; availability cannot be guaranteed.

Google Play's Pakistan payment page lists JCB, Mastercard and Visa. Photoroom says mobile subscriptions are billed through Google Play; web subscriptions use Stripe and accept most major credit/debit cards and PayPal, with PayPal availability depending on currency and account. This does **not** prove that a particular Pakistani card will pass Photoroom's checkout, so actual Photoroom billing acceptance remains unverified.

## Android status

**Official Android app verified.** Google Play lists **Photoroom: AI Photo & Video** (package com.photoroom.app), published by Photoroom AI Photo Editor. The listing showed 100M+ downloads, 4.7 stars and about 4.04M reviews; its displayed update date was 2026-11-03. It is free to install with in-app purchases. Google Play availability can vary by country.

## Quality and adoption evidence

Evidence types are kept separate:

- **Vendor claims:** Photoroom's About page reports 7 billion images processed per year, 1 million brands, 1M+ businesses and a #1 mobile-app position across 40+ countries. These are company-reported figures, not independently audited.
- **Platform adoption:** Google Play showed 100M+ downloads, 4.7 stars and about 4.04M reviews on the listing checked 2026-11-16. Store metrics indicate adoption, not a controlled quality benchmark.
- **Independent review platform:** G2 listed 4.3/5 from 20 reviews (page modified 2026-11-11). This is a small sample; the company profile is marked as claimed by Photoroom. Review themes praise ease of use and background removal, while some reviewers note imperfect cutouts and limited fine-grained editing.
- **Anecdotal community feedback:** a recent r/photoroom discussion includes a user complaint that a changed batch-to-full-editor workflow disrupted their process; other commenters report positive results. This is anecdotal and not representative.
- **Benchmarks:** no independent standardized image-editing benchmark for the current hosted product was verified.
